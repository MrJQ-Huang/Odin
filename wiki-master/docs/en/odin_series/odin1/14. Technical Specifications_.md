# 14. Technical Specifications

| Category | Parameter | Specification / Value |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **Depth Module** | Depth resolution | 240 × 180 |
|| Range (Max) | 30 m @ 10% reflectivity (< 500 lux); 70 m @ 90% reflectivity (< 500 lux) |
|| Range (Min) | 0.2 m |
|| Point rate | Up to 700,000 pts/s |
|| Range accuracy | ±3 cm @ 1&sigma; [1] |
|| Field of view (FOV, H × V) | 120° H × 90° V |
|| Angular resolution | 0.5° × 0.5° |
|| Laser wavelength | 940 nm |
|| Laser safety class | Class 1 (eye-safe) |
|| Frame rate | 10 FPS by default, up to 15 FPS |
| **RGB Camera Module** | Resolution | 1600 × 1296 |
|| Exposure | Global shutter |
|| FOV (H × V) | 129° H × 104° V × 173° D |
|| Image size | 1/2.9" |
|| Pixel size | 3 µm × 3 µm |
|| Frame rate | 10 / 15 fps |
| **System** | Built-in SLAM accuracy | ±5 cm + 1% [2] |
|| Weight | ~ 280 g |
|| Dimensions (L × W × D) | ~ 100 × 62 × 43 mm (body) / 46 mm (with aviation plug) |
|| Power consumption | 11 W typical, 24 W peak |
|| Ingress protection | IP66 |
|| Operating voltage | 9–24 V |
|| Operating temperature | -20 °C ~ 55 °C |
|| Storage temperature | -25 °C ~ 60 °C |
| **Data Output** | Point cloud | True-color SLAM point cloud / raw point cloud |
|| Image | Raw RGB image |
|| IMU | High-rate raw data |
|| Pose | High-rate rotation and position |
|| Software interface | Open-source SDK with ROS 1 / ROS 2 support |

**[1]** Indoor test environment, computed as standard deviation over 20 consecutive frames against an 80% Lambertian reflectance board. At 5 m: ±3 cm @ 1σ; from 5 m to 30 m, the 1σ accuracy stays below 1%.

**[2]** Under laboratory conditions.
