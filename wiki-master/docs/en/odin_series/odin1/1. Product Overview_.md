# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Introduction

### MindPalace Odin1: The World's First Spatial Memory Module

By deeply fusing LiDAR and vision sensors, Odin1 delivers high-precision, wide field-of-view real-time 3D perception. The built-in MindSLAM<sup>TM</sup> high-performance fusion SLAM algorithm enables persistent spatial memory, providing hippocampus-level navigation and cognition for the "robot brain". Odin1 is designed for rapid integration and deployment in robots, autonomous platforms, and intelligent edge devices, and runs reliably in complex indoor and outdoor environments.
