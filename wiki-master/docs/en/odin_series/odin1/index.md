# Odin1

| Feature | Specification |
|:---|:---|
| **Ultra-high-performance perception** | 70 m (90 % reflectivity) / 30 m (10 % reflectivity) ultra-long range · 120° × 90° ultra-wide field of view · up to 700 k points/sec high-density point cloud · color global-shutter imaging |
| **Ultra-robust spatial memory** | ±5 cm + 1 % centimeter-level global localization · up to 400 Hz pose update · self-adaptive in low-light / glass / water-surface and other complex environments |
| **Compact all-in-one industrial design** | ~280 g ultra-light · 100 × 62 × 43 mm compact size · IP66 dust-/water-proof · 12–24 V wide-voltage input · shock-resistant up to 32 G |
