# 13. Maintenance and Troubleshooting

## 13.1 Routine Maintenance

1. **Cleaning guidelines:**

   * Use a blower to remove dust from the optical window.
   * Wipe the device housing with a clean, soft cloth.
   * ⚠️ **Do not** use corrosive solvents to clean the optical window. Corrosive solvents will damage the surface coating; if any solvent seeps in through the gap around the window, it will corrode the waterproof adhesive on the back of the glass and cause the waterproof seal to fail. Forbidden corrosive solvents include but are not limited to: alcohol, isopropyl alcohol, white spirit, turpentine, etc.

2. **Storage conditions:**

   * Temperature: -20 °C ~ 55 °C | Humidity: < 85% RH
